Rules this page applies (from the LST PDF)
  1. Asia range (LST 2.0): the high and low from 7:00 PM to 2:00 AM New York time are the liquidity levels. The range is labeled small (compressed energy, best days), normal or large (energy already spent in Asia) against the last 10 days.
  2. Liquidity grab: after Asia and until 12:00 PM NY, price trades above the Asia high (looking for a SELL, M pattern) or below the Asia low (looking for a BUY, W pattern). 🔥 PRIME marks confirmations in the prime hours: London 3:00–5:00 AM and New York 9:00–11:00 AM.
  3. Confirmation (Type 2, Manipulation Liquidity), always on a closed candle: Model 1: the grab candle closes back past the line, in the opposite half of the candle, with a visible rejection wick (damage). Model 2: the next candle is an engulfing that covers at least 50% of candle #1's body and closes back past the line. Model 3: the third candle closes back past the line with a solid body.
  4. Continuation (Type 1): if no model appears and price stays beyond the level, the page labels it "Type 1" and does not alert. If price returns inside without a model, a new sweep is evaluated again.
  5. Management: entry at the close of the model candle. Stop beyond the wick that took the liquidity, with some margin (never inside the model). At 50% of the move that took the liquidity: take 25–50% partials and move to break-even (never BE without partials). Target: the next liquidity, here the opposite side of the Asia range. Leave a small "pawn" running.

Not automated (needs your eye): Fibonacci 0.5 / 0.618 entries, internal liquidity points, Setup B, the higher-timeframe trend (a model with the trend is worth more) and S/R zones. This page only sends alerts. It never places trades.
